# Case Study — Bilingual Digital Invitation (Sanitized)

## Problem
A real event required a bilingual invitation and RSVP experience that worked for guests and administrators rather than remaining a mock-up.

## Shipped work
- Live site: https://wilchi-weddinginvitation.pages.dev/
- Public source repository: https://github.com/ComnectiveSR/wilchi-wedding-invitation

The product moved through production packaging, live-friction QA, release preparation, accessibility checks, user-facing refinement, and real guest use.

## What changed after live use
The work did not end at the first deploy. Guest-flow and admin friction were turned into bounded fixes, then re-checked before wider use. Examples included RSVP-state recovery, clearer dinner-selection validation, shorter saved-confirmation flows, admin progress/error visibility, and removal of tracking-aware reminder language.

## What this proves
- shipping a user-facing artifact;
- release and QA discipline;
- real-user feedback;
- failure-to-fix iteration;
- balancing presentation with operational flows.

## Public limitation
No guest list, personal invitation token, RSVP detail, or private backend data belongs in this repository.
