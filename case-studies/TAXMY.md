# Case Study — TaxMY (Sanitized)

## Problem
A tax-readiness product needed to turn messy records into traceable state without pretending uncertain facts were known.

## Approach
The current specification emphasizes synthetic public-demo data, exception handling, human answers that change shared state, evidence traceability, hard launch tests, desktop/mobile observation and fail-safe states.

## What this proves
- shaping the build around customer outcomes;
- evidence and privacy boundaries;
- explicit acceptance criteria;
- UX + technical verification rather than code-only completion.
