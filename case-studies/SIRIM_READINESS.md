# Case Study — Manufacturing Readiness (Anonymized)

## Problem
A manufacturing/compliance engagement required fragmented requirements, documents, supplier dependencies, equipment readiness and audit preparation to become a controlled delivery plan.

## Role
Owned the engagement from client acquisition and scope through documentation architecture, process/control planning, supplier coordination, equipment/readiness planning, audit preparation, client communication and commercial follow-up, excluding the regulator's actual audit.

## What this proves
- customer discovery and commercial ownership;
- evidence/control thinking;
- multi-party implementation management;
- turning ambiguity into an inspectable operating system.
